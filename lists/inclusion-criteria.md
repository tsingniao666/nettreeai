# Inclusion criteria

This repository publishes a **small, maintained starter set**—not an exhaustive directory dump.

A destination belongs here only if it clears most of the checks below. When evidence is thin, we leave it out rather than invent confidence.

## Must have

1. **Real discovery or credibility value** for SaaS, AI tools, developer products, or startups (launch platforms, software directories, review sites, company profiles, or high-signal communities).
2. **A clear public homepage** and a plausible path for humans to list or participate (submit form, vendor signup, editorial pitch, or community post).
3. **Language / market fit** that we can state honestly (English, Chinese, Global, US, EU, China, etc.).
4. **No obvious spam farm signals**: doorway pages, sold links, cloaking, or “pay us to inject a dofollow backlink” as the primary product.

## Prefer

- Established brands or long-running catalogs with editorial or review standards.
- Transparent pricing (free entry vs paid boost)—we label both.
- Destinations where a listing can be **verified later** (a public product page you can fetch).

## Do not include

- Private invite-only lists with no public face.
- Broken / parked domains.
- Comment spam targets, PBNs, or “guaranteed ranking” services.
- Duplicates of the same root domain with no distinct workflow.

## How this relates to NetTree

The rows in [`starter-directories.md`](./starter-directories.md) are a **public preview sample**. The product at [nettree.ai](https://nettree.ai) matches a larger maintained library against your site profile, keeps human approval in the loop, and treats **Live** (verified link) differently from **Submitted** (activity only).
