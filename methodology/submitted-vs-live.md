# Submitted vs Live

> **Web version:** [submitted-vs-live.html](https://tsingniao666.github.io/nettreeai/submitted-vs-live.html)

Growth work often collapses two different facts into one:

| Status | What it means | What it does **not** mean |
|--------|---------------|---------------------------|
| **Submitted** | Your team (or a helper) completed an action—form sent, listing drafted, pitch emailed. | A crawlable link exists on the destination. |
| **Live** | A public page is reachable and contains a link (or listing) pointing at the intended target URL. | Guaranteed rankings, forever-stable placement, or a followed link attribute. |

## Why the split matters

- HTTP 200 on a directory homepage ≠ your product is listed.
- “We submitted 50 places” ≠ “we earned 50 backlinks.”
- Links disappear. A honest system can move a win back to **needs review** when verification fails.

## How NetTree uses this

NetTree assists research, matching, drafts, and common fields. Sign-in, CAPTCHA, rule checks, and the final click stay with humans. After an action is recorded as submitted, verification (fetch + link parsing, plus human review when needed) is what promotes an opportunity to **Live**.
