# Human in the loop

NetTree is built for teams that need **defendable** backlink work—not silent auto-spam.

## What AI is for

- Read a public site and draft a working profile (positioning, audiences, markets, language).
- Rank maintained destinations by fit signals you can inspect.
- Prepare editable drafts from target-page context.
- Keep rationale and missing evidence visible before something enters a queue.

## What stays human

- Account creation and sign-in on third-party sites.
- CAPTCHAs and policy confirmation.
- The final submit / publish click.
- Whether a recommendation is worth pursuing for *this* product.

## What we refuse by design

- Bulk comment / forum spam as a product feature.
- Invented quality scores when evidence is missing.
- Treating “submitted” as a ranking outcome.

If a recommendation cannot explain fit, source context, and gaps, it should not sound confident.
