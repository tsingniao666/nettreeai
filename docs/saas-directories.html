Before you submit

  1. Match the destination to your audience, language, and market.
  2. Read current submission, editorial, pricing, and community rules.
  3. Prepare product name, one-line description, category, screenshots, and target URL.
  4. Record the action as Submitted, not Live.
  5. Save the final public listing URL and verify the intended link appears.

Frequently asked questions

What makes this SaaS directory list different?

It is criteria-led and small. Every entry has a public home, a plausible human workflow, and notes on audience fit. We reject spam farms, broken domains, and uncritical bulk dumps.

Is this the full NetTree source library?

No. This is a public preview. NetTree workspaces add deeper fit evidence, saved opportunities, and verification tracking against your site profile.

How often is the list updated?

Directory links are rechecked on a schedule and changes are recorded in the changelog. Suggest additions through the repository only when they meet the inclusion criteria.
