Built-in boundaries

  • AI prepares context and drafts—humans keep judgment.
  • No invented quality score when evidence is missing.
  • Submitted ≠ Live.
  • Public preview list stays small and criteria-led.

How the loop works

One composition for the work: understand the site, match with evidence, explain the fit, then verify outcomes.

Understand

Start from a URL. Review positioning, audiences, markets, and language before recommendations run.

Match & explain

Rank destinations by fit signals and gaps—not a mystery score or a generic dump.

Prove

Record what was submitted, then verify whether a real link or listing went live.
